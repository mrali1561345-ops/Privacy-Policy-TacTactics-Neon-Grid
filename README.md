# Privacy Policy for TacTactics: Neon Grid

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

Welcome to TacTactics: Neon Grid ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, secure gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data and privacy when you play TacTactics: Neon Grid.

# 1. Information We Collect

TacTactics: Neon Grid is designed with a privacy-first, deterministic gaming philosophy.

No Personal Data Collection: We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personal identification information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Local Game Data: Game progress, tactical settings, high scores, match statistics, and preferences are stored locally on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you manually reset your app data.

Non-Personal Analytics (Optional): Basic, aggregated diagnostic or performance metrics (such as crash logs or frame rate performance) may be processed by your operating system (iOS/Android/Web platform) according to your system settings to ensure stable gameplay.

# 2. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal data:

Local game state data is used solely to save your game progress, settings, and local match records.

No data is sold, rented, shared, or traded with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

# 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Zero Ads & Zero Pay-to-Win: TacTactics: Neon Grid contains no third-party banner ads, pop-ups, video ads, or tracking software.

No Third-Party Trackers: We do not integrate tracking SDKs, social media plugins, or targeted advertising frameworks.

# 4. Children’s Privacy

TacTactics: Neon Grid is suitable for audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate appropriate action.

# 5. Security

Your local game data is stored securely within your device's standard storage directory. While no digital storage method is 100% secure, TacTactics: Neon Grid minimizes risk by avoiding external database transfers of your gameplay data.

# 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted directly within this document with an updated "Effective Date."
